== Description ==

AG Starter Avocat is a lightweight theme designed for law firms, attorneys, notaries and legal professionals who want an online presence that is ready to use out of the box. All theme strings are written in English (translation-ready) and the theme bundles a complete French (fr_FR) translation, so it works in both languages.

Main features:

* Translation-ready: English source strings with a bundled French translation (languages/fr_FR.mo). A ready-to-use .pot file is included.
* Sober, professional design: a navy-and-champagne palette that inspires trust.
* Ready to use: replace the firm name, address, business hours and practice areas in the Customizer — the rest is already in place.
* Responsive: adapts to mobile, tablet and desktop.
* Light and fast: no JavaScript dependency, instant loading, system fonts only (no Google Fonts).
* Block editor compatible (Gutenberg), with support for wide and full-width alignments.
* Custom logo, custom colors, main menu, widgets and threaded comments supported.
* No plugin required.

Pre-built content included:

* Home hero section with a subtitle and a "Book an appointment" button.
* Three cards: Practice areas, Fees, Book an appointment.
* A "The firm" section.
* Footer with the firm's contact, business hours (video consultation included) and address.

Just open the Customizer (Appearance > Customize > AG Starter — Customization) and replace the placeholder texts with your real information.

== Installation ==

1. In WordPress, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New.
2. Click "Upload Theme" and choose the ag-starter-avocat.zip file.
3. Click "Install Now" and then "Activate".
4. Create an empty page, then go to Settings > Reading and set it as your "Homepage".
5. Customize the content via Appearance > Customize.

Yes. It was designed specifically for legal professionals, with sections and texts tailored to them (practice areas, fees, confidentiality, appointment booking).

Yes, directly from Appearance > Customize > AG Starter — Customization.
